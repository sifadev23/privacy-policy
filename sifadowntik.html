Privacy Policy

SifaDownTik
Last Updated: December 29, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to SifaDownTik ("we," "our," or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our application.

SifaDownTik is a video downloading application that allows users to download videos for personal use. We designed our app with privacy in mind.

🔒 Our Privacy Commitment

We do NOT collect, store, or share your personal information. We do not have access to your name, email, phone number, contacts, or any other personal data. Your privacy is our priority.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Advertising Identifier

The only information collected by our app is your Advertising ID, which is automatically collected by Google AdMob for the purpose of showing personalized advertisements.

What is an Advertising ID?
The Advertising ID is a unique, user-resettable identifier provided by Google Play Services. It is used solely for advertising purposes and does not contain any personal information about you.

2.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

We explicitly do NOT collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data (GPS coordinates, address)
  • Device information (beyond what's required for app functionality)
  • Contacts or contact lists
  • Photos, videos, or media files (except those you choose to download)
  • Browsing history or search history
  • Payment information (handled securely by Google Play)
  • Messages or communication content

3. How We Use Information

3.1 Advertising ID Usage

The Advertising ID is used exclusively by Google AdMob to:

  • Display relevant advertisements within the app
  • Measure ad performance and effectiveness
  • Prevent fraud and ensure ad quality

Note: If you purchase the premium version of the app to remove ads, the Advertising ID will no longer be used as advertisements will be disabled.

3.2 App Functionality

The app requires the following permissions to function:

  • Internet Access: To fetch video download links
  • Storage Access: To save downloaded videos to your device

These permissions are used solely for the app's core functionality and do not involve data collection.

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Google AdMob

Our app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use your Advertising ID in accordance with Google's privacy policies.

For more information about how Google uses data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Privacy Information

4.2 Google Play Billing

For in-app purchases (premium version), we use Google Play's billing system. All payment information is handled securely by Google Play, and we do not have access to your payment details.

No Other Third Parties

We do NOT share your information with any other third-party services, analytics platforms, or data brokers. The only third-party service we use is Google AdMob for advertising.

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Local Storage Only

All videos you download are stored locally on your device. We do not upload, store, or have access to any content you download using our app.

5.2 No Cloud Storage

We do not use cloud storage or remote servers to store any user data. Everything stays on your device.

5.3 Security Measures

While we do not collect personal data, we implement industry-standard security practices to protect the app's functionality and ensure safe operation.

6. Your Privacy Rights

6.1 Advertising ID Control

You can control your Advertising ID through your device settings:

  • Reset Advertising ID: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID
  • Opt-out of Personalized Ads: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

6.2 Remove Ads Completely

You can purchase the premium version of our app for $0.99 to completely remove all advertisements and stop the use of your Advertising ID.

6.3 Delete App Data

You can delete all app data at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the app (removes all app data)
  • Settings → Apps → SifaDownTik → Storage → Clear Data

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information to us, please contact us immediately.
